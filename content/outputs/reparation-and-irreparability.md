In _Reparation and Irreparability: Theoretical Approaches_, a book that advances a theoretical perspective on cultural practices of reparation and the irreparable, Ariane Jeßulat contributed a chapter entitled _Être touché : improvisations polyphoniques et contacts humains_. 

This work discusses polyphonic improvisation techniques of the Renaissance. It focuses in particular on the _tactus_, the voluntary contact between singers, and examines musical examples where maximum proximity in imitation processes creates the strongest tensions and energies. (Note: article is in French.)
