Technologies of Touch has a paper at NIME 2026 in London. Entitled "What NIME Talks About When It Talks About Touch", this publication represents a thematic analysis of the NIME corpus 2000-2025. NIME is a community deeply engaged with music, but when we looked in the literature we could find little directly about touch. Since touch is a huge part of music we carried out this analysis to get a full picture of what NIME says on the theme of "touch".

[Read it here](https://nime.org/proceedings/2026/nime2026_5.pdf), and find ways to cite on the [Outputs]({{< relref "/outputs" >}}) page.
