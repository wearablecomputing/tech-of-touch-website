---
title: "Workshop: Ethics of Touch"
date: 2026-04-27
---

On April 27 & 28 2026 the entire Technologies of Touch team will meet in Berlin for a workshop on the Ethics of Touch.

On Day 1 we will welcome speakers who will talk on the topic of accessibility, consent and ethics. These speakers include:

- Fabio Morreale, Sony AI, Barcelona ES
- Celine Kim, mdw, Vienna AT
- Jay Pocknell, Sound Without Sight, Bristol UK

On day 2 we welcome a number of performers to the Konzertsaal at UdK, to perform and talk about the role of touch in their practice. These performers include:

- Federico Visi
- Dominique Baron-Bonarjee
- Erämaa Trio
