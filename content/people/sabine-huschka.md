PD Dr. Sabine Huschka is a Berlin-based scholar of dance and theatre studies, holding a postdoctoral qualification (Habilitation) in Theatre Studies. 
She has been a research associate on the Technologies of Touch project,  specifically the sub-project Touch and Embodiment Theory, at UdK Berlin since September 2025.
