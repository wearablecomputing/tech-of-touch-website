The Erämaa Trio was founded in 2016 by Cédric de Bruycker, Akiko Okawa and Quentin Meurisse, three musicians who graduated from the Royal Conservatory of Brussels and the Hogeschool Gent in contemporary music. Erämaa performs regularly at festivals and venues and frequently plays in Belgium, The Netherlands, France and Japan.
